    PHYTONEXUS · Digital Nervous System for Safe & Reproducible Botanical Extraction
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Abstract
    
        
    A unified, hardware-agnostic closed-loop control and telemetry layer that transforms small-scale phytochemical extraction into a monitored, safe, and reproducible process. Aligned with UN SDG 9, PHYTONEXUS bridges cheap physical components and data platforms through simulation-first onboarding, fault-tolerant state machines, and real-time process intelligence.
    
        
    Impact
    
        
    Safer workplaces · Run-to-run reproducibility · Lower entry barriers · Decentralised botanical research & micro-enterprise infrastructure.

    Problem Statement & Gaps Closed
    
    
    Botanical extraction in small-scale labs suffers from core infrastructure gaps:
    
    
    
      
    Safety Visibility Gap
    Flammable ethanol vapours near heating elements cause explosion hazards when unmonitored.
    
      
    Quality & Data Gap
    Thermal surges spoil active compounds; process telemetry is rarely recorded systematically.
    
      
    Access & Integration Gap
    Industrial extractors cost lakhs; cheap discrete components lack a common data/control layer.
    
      
    Reliability Gap
    Standard microcontrollers fail silently when sensors drift or disconnect during long runs.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    How It Works · The Closed Loop
    
    
      
      
    1. Sense — MQ-3 continuously measures ambient ethanol vapour; DHT22 samples climate conditions.
      2. 
      
    2. Decide — ESP32 state machine classifies readings into NORMAL / WARNING / ALERT / FAULT / LOCK states.
      3. 
      
    3. Act — On hazard or threshold exceedance, relay isolates the heater load in <50 ms with local visual and acoustic alarms.
      4. 
      
    4. Recover — Hysteresis recovery re-arms the heater safely only after vapour/temp returns to safe operating envelope.
      5.
